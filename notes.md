**WWE '13 running natively on a jailbroken PS5.** This is a PS5 port of [PromethenDev's WWE '13 Recomp](https://github.com/PromethenDev/wwe13-recomp). The recompilation and its game fixes are their work; this release brings it to the PS5. Go give the original project a ⭐!

## What's in it
- 720p, 1080p or 1440p, matches at 60 fps (entrances and cutscenes at 30, as on the Xbox 360) or a 30 fps lock, 4x or 2x anti-aliasing and anisotropic filtering
- PlayStation buttons in the game's prompts and a DualSense on the controller select screen (Xbox buttons are a setting away)
- Trophies: the game's achievements pop up as trophies while you play, and the hub's Trophies page tracks your progress
- A hub made for the DualSense: Home, Settings, Saves, Trophies, DLC and About pages
- DLC: install your DLC packages from the hub
- Save backups: back up and restore your saves from the hub
- Menu music: put your own songs in `hub/music/`, R3 skips to the next one

## What you need
- A jailbroken PS5 that runs homebrew apps from `/data/homebrew/`
- Your **own** copy of WWE '13 for Xbox 360 and its title update 2.0.1.0 (`default.xexp`). No game files are included.

## Install
Install it from [Recomp Store](https://recompstore.com), or download `PPSA92013.zip` below and copy the `PPSA92013` folder to `/data/homebrew/`. Then copy your WWE 13 game folder (`default.xex`, `default.xexp`, `pac`, `sound` and the rest) into `PPSA92013/Assets/`. `READ ME.txt` in the folder walks you through it.

## Known issues
- There's no Xbox LIVE, so online modes are out, and the 7 achievements that need it can't be earned (the Trophies page lists them apart).
